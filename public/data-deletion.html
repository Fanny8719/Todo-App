Todo at todo.fannycynthia.com

Delete individual tasks

Sign in and tap the bin icon next to any task. It's removed from our database right away.

Delete your whole account

  1. Send an email to fanny.cynthia@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Todo account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in, or include that address in the message.
  3. We'll permanently delete your account and all of your tasks within 30 days, and reply to confirm.

Deleting your account removes your email, name, profile photo and every task you created.

Signed in with Facebook or Google?

You can also remove Todo's access from your account settings. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites and remove Todo. On Google, go to myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party connections. This stops future sign-ins. To also erase what's already stored, send the email above.
