Privacy Policy

Todo at todo.fannycynthia.com · Last updated 13 November 2026

Todo is a simple to-do list app. This page explains what information it collects, why, and how you can have it removed.

What we collect

  • Your account details. Your email address. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we also receive your name and profile photo from that service.
  • Your tasks. The text of each task, whether it's completed, and when it was created.
  • Basic sign-in records. Our sign-in provider keeps short-lived technical logs, such as the time of sign-in and IP address, for security.

If you sign up with an email and password, your password is stored only as a secure hash. We never see it.

How we use it

Only to run the app. That means signing you in, showing you your own tasks, and keeping other people from seeing them. We don't use your information for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone except the services that run the app.

Where it's stored

  • Supabase stores your account and tasks, in Canada (ca-central-1).
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Google and Facebook handle sign-in if you choose them. Their own privacy policies apply to what happens on their sites.

Who can see your tasks

Only you. The database itself only returns tasks to the account that created them.

Deleting your data

You can delete any task at any time inside the app. To delete your whole account and everything in it, follow the steps on our data deletion page.
